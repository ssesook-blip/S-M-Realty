Casa Linda, Sosúa

Casa Linda sits in the hills above Sosúa, on the Dominican Republic's north coast — a gated, resort-style community built over more than three decades into one of the area's most established addresses. It's laid out as a self-contained neighborhood: private villas and townhomes set around shared clubhouse grounds, with its own restaurant, water park, and recreation facilities, rather than relying on anything outside the gates.

Homes in the community range from compact 2-bedroom villas to larger 5- and 6-bedroom estates, and villas typically come with their own private pool, a landscaped lot, and finishes like quartz countertops and solid wood cabinetry as standard. Several of the resale homes currently available are already fully furnished and set up as short-term rentals, so a buyer can move in — or start renting the property out — right away.

Day to day, Casa Linda runs like a small resort: 24-hour manned security at the gate, a dedicated events coordinator, and a monthly HOA fee that covers common-area landscaping, road upkeep, and the shared amenities below. It's this combination — real security, resort-style recreation, and genuinely turnkey homes — that makes Casa Linda one of the communities we get asked about most.

What's Inside the Gates

Chill & Grill Bar & Eatery

The community's own restaurant and bar, with a daily happy hour, themed dinner nights, and live music — many residents don't need to leave Casa Linda for a night out.

Waterworks Aquapark

A resort-style water park built into the grounds: an adults' pool, a lazy river, a dedicated kids' zone, and a poolside bar.

Trails & Recreation

A roughly 3 km walking trail loops the property, alongside a mini-golf course and tennis and pickleball courts.

Shuttle Service

A daily shuttle runs residents into Sosúa, out to Cabarete beach, and to nearby shopping, so a car isn't strictly necessary.

24-Hour Gated Security

Manned, gated access around the clock, with security patrolling the community grounds day and night.

Community Life

An onsite welcome center, an events coordinator, and a genuine social calendar — Casa Linda was built to feel like a neighborhood, not just a subdivision.

Buying in Casa Linda

What to know before you buy in Casa Linda

Who it suits

Casa Linda works best for buyers who want a turnkey home with security and amenities already in place: retirees and snowbirds, families who spend the winter on the North Coast, and investors who want a furnished villa that can be rented out when they're not using it. If you'd rather be walking distance from the beach and restaurants, a condo in El Batey or a home in Sosúa Ocean Village may suit you better.

Resale homes and new villas

You'll find two kinds of homes for sale here. Resale villas are often sold furnished and can be lived in or rented right away. New villa models are built to the community's standard specifications, usually with a private pool, and let you choose finishes before completion. We'll walk you through both and what each means for timing and price.

Costs beyond the purchase price

Budget for the monthly HOA fee, utilities, pool and garden care, and insurance. At closing, expect roughly 4–5% on top of the price for transfer tax and legal fees. Our closing costs and property tax guide breaks this down with a worked example.

Getting there

Casa Linda is in the hills just outside Sosúa, a short drive from Sosúa's beaches and town centre, with Cabarete and Puerto Plata's international airport (POP) both within easy reach. The community shuttle covers the daily runs into town.

Casa Linda FAQs

Is Casa Linda a gated community?

Yes. Casa Linda has manned, gated access 24 hours a day, with security patrolling the grounds day and night.

Can foreigners buy a home in Casa Linda?

Yes. Foreigners can buy property in the Dominican Republic with the same ownership rights as Dominicans. Our guide to buying as a foreigner walks through the process step by step.

How much do homes in Casa Linda cost?

Prices depend on size, finishes and whether a home is furnished. The current range, updated daily, is shown in the availability section above, from 2-bedroom villas up to 6-bedroom estates.

Do I need a car if I live in Casa Linda?

Not strictly. A daily shuttle runs residents into Sosúa, to Cabarete beach and to nearby shopping, and the community has its own restaurant, water park and recreation. Many owners still keep a car for convenience.

Can I rent out my Casa Linda villa?

Many owners do. Several homes currently for sale are already furnished and run as short-term rentals. Rental rules are set by the community, so we confirm the current rules for any home you're considering.

What does the HOA fee cover?

The monthly HOA fee covers common-area landscaping, road upkeep, 24-hour security and the shared amenities. The amount varies by property, so we share the exact fee for every home we show you.
