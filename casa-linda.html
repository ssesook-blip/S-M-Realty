Casa Linda, Sosúa

Casa Linda sits in the hills above Sosúa, on the Dominican Republic's north coast — a gated, resort-style community built over more than three decades into one of the area's most established addresses. It's laid out as a self-contained neighborhood: private villas and townhomes set around shared clubhouse grounds, with its own restaurant, water park, and recreation facilities, rather than relying on anything outside the gates.

Homes in the community range from compact 2-bedroom villas to larger 5- and 6-bedroom estates, and villas typically come with their own private pool, a landscaped lot, and finishes like quartz countertops and solid wood cabinetry as standard. Several of the resale homes currently available are already fully furnished and set up as short-term rentals, so a buyer can move in — or start renting the property out — right away.

Day to day, Casa Linda runs like a small resort: 24-hour manned security at the gate, a dedicated events coordinator, and a monthly HOA fee that covers common-area landscaping, road upkeep, and the shared amenities below. It's this combination — real security, resort-style recreation, and genuinely turnkey homes — that makes Casa Linda one of the communities we get asked about most.

What's Inside the Gates

Chill & Grill Bar & Eatery

The community's own restaurant and bar, with a daily happy hour, themed dinner nights, and live music — many residents don't need to leave Casa Linda for a night out.

Waterworks Aquapark

A resort-style water park built into the grounds: an adults' pool, a lazy river, a dedicated kids' zone, and a poolside bar.

Trails & Recreation

A roughly 3 km walking trail loops the property, alongside a mini-golf course and tennis and pickleball courts.

Shuttle Service

A daily shuttle runs residents into Sosúa, out to Cabarete beach, and to nearby shopping, so a car isn't strictly necessary.

24-Hour Gated Security

Manned, gated access around the clock, with security patrolling the community grounds day and night.

Community Life

An onsite welcome center, an events coordinator, and a genuine social calendar — Casa Linda was built to feel like a neighborhood, not just a subdivision.
