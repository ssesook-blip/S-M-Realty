Natural Habitat — 2 Bedroom Residences

Residencial Perla Marina, Sosúa — Dominican Republic

Room to breathe, steps from the sand. These 2-bedroom, 1–2 bath apartments across Natural Habitat's A and B buildings give families and investors alike real space — living room, separate dining/kitchen, private balcony — inside a gated community designed to feel like a retreat rather than a development. It's the kind of layout that works equally well as a full-time home, a snowbird escape, or a reliable rental thanks to the project's built-in, hands-off management program. Every unit already has its own individual Certificate of Title, so buyers can move with confidence.

Location: Residencial Perla Marina, Sosúa

  • 3 minutes' walk to the beach

  • 10 minutes to Cabarete & Sosúa nightlife, restaurants, yoga studios, and top kitesurf/surf spots

  • 20 minutes to Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP)

The Residence:

  • 2 Bedroom, 1–2 Bath, ~80–94 m² (balcony ~11.55 m²; penthouse units up to 16.60 m²)

  • Living room, dining/kitchen, private balcony

  • Rooftop (3rd level) access

  • Parking included

  • Smart lock entry, white-line kitchen appliances

  • Available in multiple layout variations — confirm current floor plan options with developer

The Community:

  • Gated with 24/7 security, cobblestone streets, gardened grounds, gazebo, cistern

  • Shared swimming pool & BBQ area

  • AirBnB-friendly, with exclusive on-site rental management pooling and distributing income monthly — marketing, guest support, and turnover all handled for owners

  • HOA fee: USD $60/month — covers 24/7 security, common area maintenance, landscaping, road maintenance, waste collection, and community administration

Project Status: Site excavation and preparation complete. Individual title already issued for every unit. Next construction phase begins following the sale of the first 7 apartments.
