Striking new villa with unique upscale finishes located in the newest section of Sosúa Ocean Village.

Situated on a 437 m² lot in the highly sought after gated community of Sosua Ocean VIllage, this beautifully maintained villa offers a comfortable 229 m² of living space with high-end finishes and lighting, and a beautifully-designed outdoor living area. Whether you're looking for a full-time residence, vacation home, or investment property, this home is perfectly positioned to enjoy everything the North Coast has to offer.

The villa features three spacious bedrooms, each with built-in wardrobe, two with en-suite bathrooms. The third bathroom is positioned just outside the final bedroom so it can serve as a guest bathroom as well as the bathroom for the final bedroom. Two bedrooms provide direct access to the outdoor pool area through sliding glass doors, creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living.

One of the standout features of this property is the very large open floor plan kitchen/dining/living area with beautiful European-style cabinetry and custom lighting throughout the space. You’ll love the wealth of cabinet space in the kitchen area including glass enclosures designed to highlight your accent pieces, and the large glass wall leading out to the pool area which includes an outdoor shower and a bar area, and has been landscaped to ensure your privacy. More custom lighting at the entry to the house gives you a gorgeous curb presence at night.
