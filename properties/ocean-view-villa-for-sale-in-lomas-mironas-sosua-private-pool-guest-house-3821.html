Solar Panels, Private Water Well & Additional Amenities

The villa offers several practical features that add convenience and potential energy and water independence.

Private Water Well & Cistern: The property benefits from its own private water well and a large water storage cistern, providing additional water security and convenience.

Covered Outdoor Workshop: Behind the main villa is a spacious, roof-covered, open-air workspace. This versatile area is ideal for woodworking, DIY projects, hobbies, storage, or other creative activities.

Prime Location – Lomas Mironas, Sosúa
