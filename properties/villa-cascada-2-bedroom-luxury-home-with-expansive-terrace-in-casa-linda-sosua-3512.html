Experience the ultimate tropical lifestyle at Villa Cascada , located in the prestigious Casa Linda community on the north coast of Sosúa, Dominican Republic.

  • Expertly designed to embrace a sloping lot, this 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath villa combines modern architecture with functional elegance.

  • Vaulted ceilings in the great room create an airy, open atmosphere, while the central kitchen island and ample cabinetry offer a stylish and practical space for cooking and entertaining.

  • Both bedrooms serve as serene retreats, each with its own private ensuite bathroom, complemented by a convenient half bath near the pool.

  • The expansive outdoor terrace is designed for tropical living, featuring an outdoor kitchen and bar—perfect for alfresco dining, entertaining, or relaxing while enjoying the lush surroundings.

Set within Casa Linda, residents enjoy proximity to Sosúa’s beaches, shopping, and dining, just minutes from Cabarete’s watersports and nightlife, and a short drive to Puerto Plata Airport.

Combining privacy, luxury, and the best of Caribbean indoor-outdoor living, Villa Cascada is ideal as a full-time residence, vacation home, or investment property.
