Exceptional opportunity to acquire Villa Estrella del Caribe, a spacious Mediterranean-style residence located within Sea Horse Ranch, one of the Dominican Republic's most established gated beachfront communities.

Positioned on an elevated 2,752 m² lot surrounded by mature tropical landscaping, this 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom villa offers exceptional space, architectural character, and long-term value for buyers seeking a property they can personalize and make their own.

The home features 540 m² of construction across two levels, highlighted by vaulted ceilings, beautiful tongue-and-groove treated pine woodwork in excellent condition, Mexican-style tile flooring, and generously proportioned living spaces that flow naturally between indoor and outdoor areas.

Each of the four bedrooms includes an ensuite bathroom, providing comfort and privacy for family members and guests alike. Outside, the property offers a private swimming pool with a sunken bar, jacuzzi, gazebo, and expansive tropical gardens, creating a peaceful setting for entertaining or enjoying the Caribbean lifestyle.

While the kitchen, bathrooms, and windows would benefit from modernization, the villa's solid structure, timeless architecture, and desirable location provide an excellent foundation for renovation, customization, and future value appreciation.
