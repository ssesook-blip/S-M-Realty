Modern Newly Built Home with Ocean Views Between Sosúa & Cabarete, Dominican Republic

Discover the perfect blend of modern Caribbean living, convenience, and potential with this newly constructed home ideally located on the beautiful North Coast of the Dominican Republic.

Situated just 5 minutes from Sosúa and 10 minutes from downtown Cabarete, this property offers an excellent location close to stunning beaches, restaurants, shopping, international schools, and the many lifestyle attractions that make this area one of the most desirable destinations in the Caribbean.

Whether you're looking for a permanent residence, a vacation home, or a Caribbean real estate investment, this property offers an exciting opportunity to create your own tropical retreat.

Modern Living with Room to Personalize

Designed with comfortable contemporary living in mind, the home features modern construction and comes equipped with installed air-conditioning units and ceiling fans for year-round comfort.

The property is being offered unfurnished, allowing the new owner to furnish and decorate the interior according to their personal style.

Spacious 100 m² Rooftop with Beautiful Ocean Views

One of the property's most attractive features is its impressive 100 m² rooftop with beautiful ocean views.

Infrastructure has already been installed to accommodate the future construction of a rooftop terrace, offering the new owner the opportunity to transform this expansive space into a spectacular outdoor living area.

Imagine enjoying your morning coffee with ocean views, entertaining friends and family under the Caribbean sky, or relaxing in the evening while watching the sunset.

The rooftop offers tremendous potential for creating a private lounge, outdoor dining area, or an elegant tropical entertainment space.

Prime Location Between Sosúa and Cabarete

Enjoy the convenience of living between two of the Dominican Republic's most popular North Coast destinations.

  • 5 minutes to Sosúa: Beautiful beaches, restaurants, supermarkets, shopping, and everyday conveniences.

  • 10 minutes to downtown Cabarete: An internationally recognized destination for kiteboarding, surfing, watersports, beachfront dining, and a vibrant international community.

  • Approximately 5 minutes to the nearest beaches: Enjoy swimming, sunbathing, water sports, and the relaxed Caribbean lifestyle.

  • Nearby international schools and everyday amenities: An attractive location for families, retirees, and international residents.

The area offers an exceptional combination of tropical beauty, recreational opportunities, and convenient access to everything needed for comfortable everyday living.
