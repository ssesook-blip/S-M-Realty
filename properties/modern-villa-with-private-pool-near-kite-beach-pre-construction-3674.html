Kite Beach Avenue is a gated villa community located on the second line of Kite Beaach in Cabarete.

Rather than a high-density condo project, this is a more residential setting made up of private two-story villas, designed with light, airflow, and outdoor living in mind.

The community offers:

– Gated entry

– 24/7 security

– Private individual lots

– Clean, modern architecture

– A structured pre-construction payment plan

It’s a more balanced approach — offering both privacy and proximity.

And yes, it also includes legally titled shared beach access, which adds an extra layer of long-term value.

AVAILABLE MODELS

All villas feature an L-shaped two-story layout that wraps around a private pool, creating a natural indoor–outdoor flow.

– Model A – 3 bedrooms / 3.5 bathrooms – Approx. 290 m² – USD $590,000

A more compact and efficient layout, ideal for buyers who want a clean, functional villa with lower entry pricing while still enjoying all the key features of the project.

– Model B – 3 bedrooms / 3.5 bathrooms – Approx. 305 m² – USD $625,000

Offers a slightly larger footprint with more generous indoor–outdoor living space, plus added architectural elements like a Brazilian oak pergola that enhance the overall feel of the home.

– Model C – 4 bedrooms / 4.5 bathrooms – Approx. 330 m² – USD $685,000

The largest option, designed for buyers who want additional bedroom capacity and more expansive living areas, making it especially well-suited for families or stronger rental potential.
