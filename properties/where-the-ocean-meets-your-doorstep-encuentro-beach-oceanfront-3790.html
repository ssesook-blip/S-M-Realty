Stunning Oceanfront 2BR/2.5BA Apartment

Playa Encuentro, Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic

2 Bedrooms | 3 Beds | 2.5 Baths | 110 sqm (approx. 1,076 sq ft) | $450,000

Directly beachfront with unobstructed ocean views from the property — this apartment is literally steps from the sand on world-famous Encuentro Beach, a bucket-list destination for surfers and one of the most sought-after stretches of coastline on the north coast. Step outside and you're on the sand — that's how close this unit sits to the water.

Along with its sister unit, this is one of only two units in the entire complex with a private patio offering direct access to the yard — a standout feature not found in any other unit in the building, ground floor included.

Interior decor and furnishings, including the sofas, were handpicked and imported from Tulum, giving the apartment a curated, distinctive aesthetic throughout. The kitchen also includes added cabinetry for enhanced storage.

The primary bedroom includes a king bed, while the second bedroom offers a queen and twin bunk arrangement, well suited to families or groups traveling together. The bright, open living and kitchen area extends to a generously sized oceanfront patio.

Buyers have the opportunity to purchase under CONFOTUR (Law 158-01), the Dominican tax incentive program that can exempt qualifying tourism properties from the 3% property transfer tax and up to 15 years of annual property tax — a valuable advantage for investment buyers.

  • Beachside and courtyard pools with lounge seating and covered conversation areas

  • Dedicated full-time concierge service

As part of a newer building, this unit is also building a strong rental track record — an appealing option for buyers looking for growing income potential.
