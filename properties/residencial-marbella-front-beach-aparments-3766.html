Residencial Marbella is a new development of 8 oceanfront apartments in Playa Chiquita, Sosúa—just steps from Ocean Village. It’s ideal for those looking for comfort, ocean views, and a peaceful Caribbean lifestyle.

The 4-story building features a modern design, swimming pool, lobby, and secure enclosed parking. Each apartment includes 2 bedrooms (the primary bedroom with a walk-in closet), 2 full bathrooms and 1 half bathroom, a living room, dining area, kitchen, and balcony.

The 4th-floor units also include an approximately 50 m² private rooftop terrace, with pre-installation for a jacuzzi, gazebo, and an additional half bathroom.
