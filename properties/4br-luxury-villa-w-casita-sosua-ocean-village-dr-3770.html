A Rare Retreat on Sosúa's Prized North Coast. This nearly completed 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom luxury villa in the prestigious gated community of Sosua Ocean Village (SOV) offers a rare opportunity to personalize your dream home just steps from the ocean — moments from Playa Laguna and Playa Chiquita, two of the North Coast's most sought-after beaches.

Set on a generous 1,200 m² lot, the residence features a dramatic two-story living room, an open-concept kitchen and dining area, and five private covered terraces designed for Caribbean indoor-outdoor living. Natural light pours through oversized windows, framing sweeping views of the landscaped grounds and pool.

The villa includes a separate one-bedroom guest casita with a private bathroom and kitchenette — ideal for extended family, visiting guests, or high-demand Airbnb stays. A beautiful pool with an overflow jacuzzi anchors the outdoor living space, creating a serene luxury retreat perfect for gatherings and relaxation. The estate also offers a one-car garage plus two additional covered parking bays.

Located only three rows from the ocean, the home enjoys partial ocean views and quick walking access to SOV's resort-style amenities. Owners enjoy 24/7 security, pristine common areas, and a long list of on-site experiences — all just minutes from Sosúa town and a short drive to Cabarete and Puerto Plata.

With outstanding rental potential and the freedom to select your own finishes, this luxury villa invites customization while delivering the privacy, security, and elevated lifestyle that define Sosua Ocean Village. Estimated completion: August 2026.
