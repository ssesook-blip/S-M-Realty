Perched on the top floor of the prestigious Cream Building at Sosúa Ocean Village, this beautifully designed two-level penthouse delivers 165 sqm of bright, open living plus a 40 sqm terrace built for Caribbean sunsets. Soaring ceilings and pocket sliding doors blend the gourmet kitchen, dining, and living areas seamlessly with the outdoors, while the main-level primary suite offers a private balcony, walk-in closet, and en-suite bath alongside a second guest bedroom and 3.5 baths.

Upstairs, a separate 1-bedroom studio with kitchenette adds a home theater and professional office — perfect as a private guest retreat or income-generating rental unit. Fully air-conditioned with in-unit washer/dryer and full-size modern appliances throughout, this move-in-ready penthouse suits full-time living, a vacation getaway, or a high-performing investment property in one of Sosúa's most sought-after communities.
