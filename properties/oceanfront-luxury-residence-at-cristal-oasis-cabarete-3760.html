Experience elevated beachfront living in this exceptional 225 sqm (2,500 sq ft) oceanfront residence at Cristal Oasis, where breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and resort-style pool create the perfect Caribbean retreat.

Featuring 3 spacious bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and an additional half bath, this beautifully designed home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and functionality in one of Cabarete's most prestigious beachfront communities.

The open-concept layout seamlessly connects the bright living and dining areas to a modern kitchen equipped with upgraded black appliances and ample cabinetry—ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A dedicated hallway office provides the perfect work-from-home space, while a separate laundry room with a stackable washer and dryer adds everyday convenience.

One of the property's most exclusive features is its private in-unit elevator, secured with a locking security door, offering unmatched privacy and effortless access. Designed for year-round comfort, the residence also includes two central air-conditioning units, ceiling fans throughout, and solar panels that improve energy efficiency and help reduce operating costs.

Residents enjoy first-class amenities, including two underground parking spaces, a private storage room, 24-hour security, and a full-building backup generator that ensures uninterrupted comfort. Outside, a spectacular 70-foot beachfront saltwater swimming pool offers the ultimate resort-style lifestyle just steps from the ocean.

Perfectly located in the heart of Cabarete, you'll enjoy direct beach access and be just a short walk from world-class restaurants, cafés, shopping, and vibrant nightlife—offering the ideal combination of luxury, convenience, and Caribbean charm.
