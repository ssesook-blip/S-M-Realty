CRISTAL OASIS

Cabarete Beach — Dominican Republic

Offered at USD $1,000,000


Ground Floor. Ocean Front. Yours.

Directly on the sand in the center of Cabarete, Cristal Oasis is a brand-new beachfront condominium where the ocean isn't a view from a balcony — it's outside your sliding glass doors. This ground-floor, 2,152-square-foot residence opens straight onto the pool deck. No stairs, no compromise, just steps to the water.

A tax advantage that closes the moment title transfers. Buy before that happens, and it transfers with you.


The Address

  • Cristal Oasis Condominium, center of Cabarete Beach

  • Ground floor, direct access to the pool deck

  • Directly on the ocean

  • Steps from Cabarete's restaurants, beach clubs, and kite culture

The Residence

Turnkey, ground-floor living built for the way people actually use a beach home.

  • 2,152 sqft (~200sqm)

  • 2 bedrooms, plus a flexible 3rd bedroom currently opened into an entertainment lounge (pool table, dartboard, pull-out sofa bed) — reconvertible to a 3rd bedroom

  • 3 full bathrooms

  • 2 assigned parking spaces

  • Large, locked private storage room in the underground garage

  • Fully furnished, brand new condition

The Building

  • Large pool and jacuzzi

  • 24/7 security

  • Fitness center in the underground parking level

  • Solar power — electric bills run remarkably low

  • Built by George Coutu, a name well known across the North Coast, and his daughter Ele — both keeping condos in the building for themselves (George in the penthouse, Ele as her home), and both sitting on the HOA

The Tax Advantage

Cristal Oasis carries a Confotur exemption with 12 years of property tax relief remaining. It only transfers to a new owner through a pre-title sale — once the current owner receives title, the exemption locks to them alone. Buy now, and the next owner inherits a benefit that otherwise disappears for good.


The Numbers

  • HOA: ~$350/month

  • Rentals permitted; comparable units in the building rent for $5,000–$8,000/month

Why Cabarete

Cabarete is the North Coast's best-known beach town — established restaurants, beach clubs, a global kitesurfing scene, and steady rental demand from a mix of long-stay expats and short-term visitors. Cristal Oasis puts a brand-new, ground-floor oceanfront residence at the center of it.
