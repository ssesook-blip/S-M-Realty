Exceptional opportunity to own a fully furnished 2-bedroom condominium in Hillside Condos, located within the highly desirable gated community of Residencial Hispaniola in Sosúa.

Completed in 2024 and never rented, this third-floor residence offers 118.31 m² of thoughtfully designed living space, combining modern comfort, privacy, and convenience in one of Sosúa's most accessible residential communities.

One of only two units featuring this unique floor plan, the condo offers high ceilings, an extended kitchen island, abundant natural light, and a spacious open-concept layout. The balcony overlooks open farmland, creating a peaceful setting to enjoy the tropical surroundings and refreshing breezes.

The fully equipped kitchen features modern cabinetry, an electric cooktop, refrigerator, built-in oven, microwave, and an oversized island perfect for entertaining. The kitchen and second bedroom also enjoy partial ocean views, adding to the appeal of this unique unit.

The second bedroom currently includes a bunk bed configuration, with the flexibility to convert the space into a home office, guest room, or children's room. Additional space throughout the unit offers the opportunity to add further storage or custom closets if desired.

As a never-rented property, the condominium remains in excellent condition and is move-in ready for full-time living, seasonal use, or investment purposes.
