The 2-Bedroom Collection at DQA Stage 3 | From $259,000

Sosúa, Dominican Republic — Space, Privacy, and Resort Amenities for Families

For buyers who need more room without leaving the DQA lifestyle behind, Block B delivers the largest floor plan in Stage 3 — and only two remain.

Available units:

  • 1B (145 m² / 1,555 ft², 1st floor) — wheelchair-accessible, main bedroom + second bedroom, main bathroom + second bathroom + guest bathroom, gallery, full kitchen, laundry room.

  • 2B (145 m² / 1,555 ft², 2nd floor) — same spacious two-bedroom layout with elevated views.

(A third unit in this block, 3B, has already sold.)

What sets this collection apart: a genuine two-bedroom footprint with a dedicated guest bathroom and gallery space — rare in Sosúa's new-construction market — plus one parking space per unit and full-size kitchen.

Same resort access as every DQA owner: two free years at the Health Club (gym, sports pool, sauna, massage room), the Casa Club (music hall, bar, game room), and the community's Mini Zoo — all just steps from your door.

Location: minutes to Sosúa's beaches and town center, 10 km to Gregorio Luperón International Airport.

Payment structure: 60% on signing, 20% at gray-stage completion, 10% at key delivery, 10% at title transfer. Reservation holds run $10,000–$15,000 (non-refundable, 30–60 days). Monthly HOA is $254. Buyer covers legal fees ($2,000–$3,000), 3% transfer tax, and any bank fees.

With one unit already sold and only two left, this is the collection to move fastest on for a buyer who wants space and privacy in a resort setting.
