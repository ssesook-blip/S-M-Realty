Residencial Sofía is a new development of modern apartments in Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic, featuring 3 four-story buildings with a total of 12 apartments. Each building offers its own layout, with 2- or 3-bedroom options and two floor-level choices: middle-floor apartments or 4th-floor units with exclusive rooftop rights, providing additional private space.
