Live Well. Live in Grace.

Steps from Kite Beach, tucked inside the quiet, coveted enclave of Costa Azul, Grace Villas is a rare thing on the North Coast: a boutique collection of only 10 contemporary loft residences, spread across 5 architect-designed buildings. No towers. No crowds. Just ten homes, done right.

This is low-density living for people who want more than a unit — they want a place. A 500-meter walk to the sand. A front-row seat to a golf course rising next door. And an address that will only get harder to come by.

Only ten residences. Intentionally limited.

THE DESIGN

Every townhome is split into two private loft residences — a layout that trades density for volume, privacy, and architectural presence. It's a design language built around light and air:

  • Double-height spaces that let the room breathe

  • Clean, modern lines

  • Indoor-outdoor flow that erases the line between the two

  • True cross-ventilation and natural light

  • A low-density footprint, by design

  • Engineered perimeter systems for privacy and structural stability

The result is architecture that feels current — built for a coastal lifestyle, not just placed in one.
