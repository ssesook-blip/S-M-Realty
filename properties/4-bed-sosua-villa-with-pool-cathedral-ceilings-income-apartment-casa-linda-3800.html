Discover space, style, and income potential in this exceptional 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom residence located in Phase 5 of Casa Linda, one of Sosua's most sought-after gated communities on the Dominican Republic's North Coast.

Ideally positioned just five minutes from grocery stores, the International School, Sosua's renowned 1-kilometer stretch of sandy beach, and a lively selection of international dining and bars, this home offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience.

Step inside to a bright, open-concept living and dining area crowned by dramatic cathedral ceilings, flowing seamlessly into a fully equipped chef's kitchen outfitted with premium modern appliances — ideal for entertaining or everyday family life.

The thoughtfully designed layout includes four generously sized bedrooms and four full bathrooms plus a convenient guest powder room. The primary suite impresses with an oversized en-suite bathroom and a custom-fitted walk-in dressing room.

Outdoor living takes center stage on the expansive covered rear terrace, overlooking a sparkling swimming pool and built-in BBQ area — a true entertainer's retreat for warm days and breezy tropical evenings.

A standout feature of this property is the private one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment on the second floor, complete with its own balcony and separate entrance — offering excellent short- or long-term rental income potential for savvy investors or multigenerational families.

Additional highlights include a dedicated laundry/utility room with washer and dryer, central air conditioning throughout, full furnishings, and ample off-street parking.
