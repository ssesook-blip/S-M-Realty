Modern 5-Bedroom House with Pool, Garden & Flexible Living

Discover this spacious 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom house located in one of the quietest and most secure residential areas of Puerto Plata. Designed across three levels, this property combines classic Spanish-style architecture on the exterior with a more modern and functional interior, offering an excellent balance between character, comfort, and versatility.

Set on a 715 m² lot with approximately 500 m² of construction, the house provides generous indoor and outdoor spaces, making it ideal for a large family, a private residence, or an investment property with rental potential.

Designed for Flexibility

The three-level layout creates excellent separation between the main living spaces and guest accommodations. This allows the property to work particularly well for owners who want to live comfortably while also generating rental income, whether through long-term tenants, guests, or short-term accommodations.

The generous outdoor areas, garden, pool, and parking provide the space and privacy expected from a premium Puerto Plata residence.

Prime Puerto Plata Location

The villa is strategically located just minutes from some of Puerto Plata's most popular beaches and essential services:

  • Approximately 5 minutes to Long Beach

  • Approximately 5 minutes to Cosita Rica Beach

  • Approximately 5 minutes to major supermarkets, including La Sirena and Jumbo

  • Approximately 15 minutes to the future Bravo supermarket

  • Approximately 8 minutes to Puerto Plata's historic city center, with its restaurants, historic architecture, culture, and local attractions

This location gives you the best of both worlds: a peaceful residential environment while remaining close to the beach, shopping, dining, and the cultural heart of Puerto Plata.

A Home with Investment Potential

With its size, five bedrooms, three levels, private pool, large garden, and flexible guest accommodations, this house offers considerably more than a traditional family home.

It can serve as a full-time residence, Caribbean vacation home, multi-generational property, or income-producing investment.

A rare combination of space, privacy, location, and flexibility in Puerto Plata.
