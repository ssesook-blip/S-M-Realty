Beautiful Oceanfront 2BR/2.5BA Unit


Playa Encuentro, Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic


2 Bedrooms | 3 Beds | 2.5 Baths | 110 sqm (approx. 1,184 sq ft) | $450,000

Directly beachfront with unobstructed ocean views from the property — this unit sits literally steps from the sand on Encuentro Beach, one of the most celebrated surf breaks in the Caribbean and a world-renowned destination for surfers, kiteboarders, and beach lovers alike. Step out onto your patio and the ocean is right there — no shuttles, no long beach walks, just sand at your doorstep.

This home is one of only two units in the entire complex with a private patio offering direct access to the yard — a rare feature not available in any other unit in the building, ground floor included.

The interior is fully furnished with handpicked decor and furnishings imported from Tulum, including the sofas, lending the space a distinct coastal-boho character you won't find in a typical rental. The kitchen has been upgraded with additional cabinetry for extra storage and functionality.

The primary bedroom is furnished with a king bed; the second bedroom offers a queen and twin bunk configuration, comfortably accommodating families or small groups. The open-concept living area connects to the fully equipped kitchen, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining.

Buyers have the opportunity to purchase under CONFOTUR (Law 158-01), the Dominican tax incentive program that can exempt qualifying tourism properties from the 3% property transfer tax and up to 15 years of annual property tax — a significant advantage for an investment property like this one.
