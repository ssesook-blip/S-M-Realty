Built by surfers and inspired by the way people actually live near the ocean, Origen Residences is not simply another modern project located near a surf beach. The surf lifestyle shaped the design from the beginning.

Located just 350 meters from Playa Encuentro, Origen is a boutique pre-construction project of only 11 two-bedroom townhouses. After a morning in the water, you can rinse off in your private outdoor shower, store your boards and equipment in the dedicated locker, and walk directly into a home designed for relaxed indoor-outdoor living.

Each two-level residence offers 110.66 m² of interior space, plus approximately 32.3 to 37 m² of terrace space. The main floor features an open-concept kitchen, dining and living area that flows directly onto the private terrace, garden, barbecue area and pool. A guest bathroom and separate laundry area complete the first floor.

Upstairs are two spacious bedrooms, both with private bathrooms and balconies. The layout gives each bedroom its own sense of privacy, making the home practical for full-time living, extended stays or hosting family and friends.

The finishes continue the natural coastal feel of the project, combining Spanish porcelain flooring, coralina stone, warm wood details and clean modern lines. The kitchen includes modular cabinetry and granite countertops, while the bathrooms feature granite-topped vanities and tempered-glass shower enclosures.
