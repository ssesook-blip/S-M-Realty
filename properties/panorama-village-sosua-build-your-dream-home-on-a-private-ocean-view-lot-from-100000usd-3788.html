Our Take

Panorama Village is where we send buyers who want ocean views and space but don't want to be in the middle of town. The lots are larger than most in Sosúa, the community is gated, and you can either pick one of the ready-designed home models or build your own. Budget for construction on top of the lot price and the monthly HOA, and plan on a timeline of a year or more for a custom build. If a quiet hillside home with a view is the goal, this is one of the better starting points around Sosúa.

Sheena Sesook & Michael Galea, S&M Realty DR

Tucked into the hills above Sosúa, Panorama Village is where a simpler pace of living meets the raw beauty of the Caribbean coastline. Here, life slows down — mornings open onto sweeping ocean views, evenings close with sunsets that never get old, and the endless spring climate means the outdoors is never far from reach.

Lots here are a step above the typical Sosúa lot: generously sized, framed by mature trees, and positioned to give owners real privacy along with some of the most striking views in the region. Buyers can choose from a selection of ready-built home models or start from scratch and design their own retreat from the ground up.

Panorama Village is built for peace of mind as much as it is for scenery: 24-hour security, an on-site aqueduct system, internet access, cable TV, and a backup emergency generator all come standard for owners. It's a community designed for anyone looking to slow down, reconnect with nature, and put down roots minutes from the beach.
