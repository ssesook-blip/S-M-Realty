Residencial Olas del Atlántico — Modern Coastal Living in Puerto Plata

Discover Residencial Olas del Atlántico, an exclusive residential project in Jardines del Atlántico, Puerto Plata, designed for buyers looking for modern comfort, proximity to the ocean, and long-term value.

Located just 450 meters from the Puerto Plata Malecón and the Atlantic Ocean, and close to Avenida Luis Ginebra, the project places residents within easy reach of the city's beaches, restaurants, services, and everyday conveniences. (Olas del Atlántico)

Project Status

The project is currently in the finishing stage, with the main structure completed and delivery scheduled for 2026. The development consists of 16 apartments across two five-level buildings, with elevators and a carefully planned distribution designed to provide privacy, functionality, and comfortable access. (Olas del Atlántico)

Modern Apartments Designed for Everyday Living

The standard apartments offer approximately 116–131 m², depending on the building and configuration, with a practical layout that includes:

  • 3 bedrooms

  • 2 bathrooms

  • Master bedroom with private bathroom and walk-in closet

  • Spacious living and dining areas

  • Modern kitchen

  • Laundry area

  • Private balcony

  • Assigned parking

  • High-quality finishes and contemporary design

The project incorporates premium materials and finishes, including porcelain flooring, quality cabinetry, modern lighting, European-style windows and doors, and natural stone finishes in selected areas. (Murcia Group)

Exclusive Penthouse Experience

For buyers looking for additional space, the fourth-level apartments offer access to private galleries on the fifth level, creating a unique penthouse-style configuration.

These exclusive areas provide additional space for a private lounge, outdoor entertaining, kitchen/barbecue area, bathroom, and flexible lifestyle uses. The penthouses also include two parking spaces, adding another layer of convenience and value. (Olas del Atlántico)

Resort-Style Amenities

Residents will have access to a collection of amenities designed to complement the coastal lifestyle:

  • 🏊 Swimming pool

  • 🏋️ Fully equipped gym

  • 🛗 Modern elevators

  • 🔐 Controlled access

  • 📹 Video surveillance

  • 🚗 Visitor parking

  • 🌴 Landscaped common areas

  • 💧 Water storage infrastructure

  • 📱 Modern intercom/access systems

These features create a residential environment that combines security, convenience, and the relaxed atmosphere expected from life near the Caribbean coast. (Olas del Atlántico)

Location That Works for Lifestyle & Investment

One of the project's strongest advantages is its location.

Being approximately 450 meters from the Malecón and Atlantic Ocean puts residents close to one of Puerto Plata's defining attractions while maintaining the convenience of an established residential neighborhood. (Olas del Atlántico)

From here, owners can enjoy easy access to:

  • 🌊 Puerto Plata Malecón

  • 🏖️ Atlantic beaches

  • 🍽️ Restaurants and local dining

  • 🛍️ Shopping and everyday services

  • 🏙️ Puerto Plata city center

  • 🌴 Major tourist and recreational attractions

A Smart Opportunity in Puerto Plata

Whether you're looking for a primary residence, Caribbean second home, vacation property, or investment, Olas del Atlántico offers a compelling combination of modern construction, coastal proximity, practical layouts, and resort-style amenities.

With the structure already completed and the project now entering its finishing phase, buyers have the opportunity to secure a property in an established development while it moves toward its 2026 delivery.
