Just a five-minute walk from Playa Encuentro, the North Coast's most famous surf beach, this two-level penthouse puts you steps from the waves. It sits within Residencial Aris and has sweeping views of the ocean, the mountains and the jungle.

It's sold furnished. Buyers can move straight in or start renting it out from day one, whether it's a family home, a vacation retreat or a short-term rental.

Property Details
Bedrooms: 4 (3+1) | Bathrooms: 3 | Living Space: 213 m² | Levels: 3rd and 4th floors | Furnished: Yes | Rooftop: Private terrace

The Complex
Residencial Aris is a quiet residential community set among lush tropical gardens. Residents share a large swimming pool with a covered bar and seating area, a natural spot to unwind or meet neighbors. HOA fees cover upkeep of the shared areas and services.

Modern Comforts and Security
A backup generator and a private water well keep power and water running without interruption. The development also has security and parking.

Inside the Apartment
The third floor is the main living level. An open-concept layout joins the living room, dining area and large kitchen, and brings in plenty of natural light, airflow and ocean views. A spacious private balcony runs off the living area and is also reached from the primary bedroom and a second bedroom. It's ideal for outdoor meals or enjoying the Caribbean breeze.

Upstairs, the fourth level holds an additional bedroom that opens onto a large private rooftop terrace. With views stretching across sea, mountains and jungle, the rooftop is a blank canvas. It's ready for a jacuzzi, an outdoor kitchen, a barbecue, a lounge or a space for entertaining.

Life in Playa Encuentro
Encuentro draws surfers from around the world for its consistent waves and relaxed community. The area has grown into a lifestyle destination built around wellness, outdoor sports and healthy living. Encuentro Plaza, a boutique community hub, is nearby. So are Bliss Restaurant Lounge, Eze Beach Bar, Vibez Cabarete and Surf & Roll. Cabarete is five minutes away by car, Sosúa about 10, and Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP) 25–30.
