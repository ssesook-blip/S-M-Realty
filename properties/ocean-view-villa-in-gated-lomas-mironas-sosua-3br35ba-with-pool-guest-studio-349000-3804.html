Wake up to Caribbean ocean views from nearly every room in this 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom villa, tucked inside the gated community of Lomas Mironas in Sosúa, Dominican Republic. Listed at $349,000 USD, it's a strong fit for anyone weighing a retirement move to Sosúa, a second home on the north coast, or a rental-ready property in one of the DR's most sought-after beach towns.

Inside, the layout keeps living, dining and kitchen space open and connected, then extends outward to a wraparound ocean-view balcony. Each bedroom opens onto its own private terrace, so the indoor-outdoor lifestyle isn't limited to one spot in the house. A fully fenced yard wraps around the pool — freshly resurfaced and repainted in 2026 — and the grounds are planted with banana, lime and avocado trees that are already mature and producing. Downstairs, a generous studio apartment with a kitchenette and full bath gives the home real flexibility, whether that's hosting visitors, housing extended family, or renting it out separately.

The roof was replaced in 2025 and still carries two years of warranty coverage, so that's one major system a buyer won't need to think about for a while.

Lomas Mironas itself runs on a low HOA fee that covers round-the-clock security, water, trash service and upkeep of shared areas — the kind of setup that makes ownership here genuinely low-maintenance. And despite the gated privacy, you're only a short drive from Sosúa and Cabarete, with beaches, restaurants and grocery stores all close by.
