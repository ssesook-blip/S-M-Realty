Exceptional opportunity to acquire Villa Agua Marina 31A, a beautifully upgraded two-level villa offering modern comfort, quality finishes, and future expansion potential within one of Sosúa's most sought-after residential communities.

This inviting residence features 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, with both bedrooms located on the second level. A spacious upstairs balcony offers the possibility of being converted into a third bedroom, home office, or additional living area with minimal investment, creating added flexibility and value for future owners.

The villa enjoys peaceful mountain views and partial ocean views, creating a bright and relaxing atmosphere throughout the property. Tasteful upgrades throughout the home include quality furnishings, elegant feature walls, and carefully selected finishes that combine comfort and modern Caribbean style.

Outside, the property is surrounded by beautifully maintained landscaping and a manicured garden. Coralina-accented exterior walls add warmth and character, while the private outdoor living area is designed for both relaxation and entertaining.
