Modern 3-Bedroom Luxury Villa for Sale in Sosúa Ocean Village, Dominican Republic

Positioned in the newest section of the prestigious gated community of Sosúa Ocean Village, this newly constructed 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom villa delivers contemporary Caribbean living at its finest. Situated on the scenic North Coast of the Dominican Republic, this striking residence spans 229.5 m² (2,470 sq. ft.) of refined living space on a generous 437 m² private lot. Designed specifically for international buyers seeking an upscale primary home, a tranquil vacation retreat, or a turnkey investment property with high rental yield potential, this home combines architectural elegance with resort-style amenities just minutes from Sosúa and Cabarete.

Owner financing available — ask us for details!

Sophisticated Open-Concept Interior & European-Style Kitchen

The heart of the home is an expansive open floor plan that seamlessly brings together the kitchen, dining, and living zones under custom designer lighting. Clean architectural lines and premium finishes elevate the entire space. Culinary enthusiasts will appreciate the gourmet kitchen, which features sleek European-style cabinetry, an abundance of storage, and illuminated glass display enclosures to showcase fine glassware and accent pieces. A floor-to-ceiling glass wall frames lush views of the private backyard, bathing the interior in natural Caribbean sunlight throughout the day.

Private Bedrooms & Well-Appointed Bathrooms

The villa offers three spacious bedrooms, each fitted with custom built-in wardrobes for streamlined storage. Two bedrooms feature private en-suite bathrooms and sliding glass doors that open directly to the swimming pool terrace, making morning swims effortless. The third full bathroom is conveniently located just outside the final bedroom, serving dual purposes as a comfortable ensuite for guests or a stylish powder room for visiting company.

Private Outdoor Oasis & Entertaining Area

Step outside into an outdoor entertaining space framed by dense tropical landscaping that ensures absolute privacy. The backyard features a private swimming pool, sun deck, built-in outdoor bar, and a dedicated outdoor shower for rinsing off after a day at the beach. Custom exterior lighting accents the front entry and rear terrace, giving the villa a striking curb presence both day and night.

World-Class Community Amenities at Sosúa Ocean Village

As a property owner in Sosúa Ocean Village, you gain access to the North Coast’s most comprehensive master-planned community infrastructure:

  • Direct beach access and scenic coastal walking and jogging trails

  • Oceanfront infinity pool and recreation at Clubhouse Al Porto

  • Olympic-sized lap pool and oceanfront fitness wellness center

  • Two on-site oceanfront restaurants and beachfront bars

  • Caribbean’s largest man-made saltwater lagoon and full access to Laguna City amenities

  • Football field, basketball courts, and nearby paddle courts

  • Adult and children’s water parks with dedicated play areas

  • Community car wash facility

  • Owner privileges: complimentary access to Clubhouse Al Porto and the Olympic pool, plus exclusive discounts on dining, gym memberships, and water park passes

Prime North Coast Location & Everyday Convenience

Located along the highway between Sosúa and the kite-surfing capital of Cabarete, this home provides quick access to everything you need:

  • International Transit: Approximately 15 minutes to Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP)

  • Essential Services: Minutes to international schools, bilingual medical centers, pharmacies, and major supermarkets (Super Pola, Playero)

  • Utilities & Infrastructure: Community-wide backup power generator, round-the-clock gated security, and high-speed fiber-optic internet connection
