Exceptional opportunity to acquire Villa La Paz, a thoughtfully designed single-level villa offering privacy, comfort, and outstanding future expansion potential within one of the North Coast's most desirable residential communities.


Set on an expansive 1,068 m² lot, this 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom villa combines functional living spaces with beautiful natural surroundings, making it ideal as a full-time residence, vacation home, or investment property.


The home's open-concept living and dining area flows seamlessly onto the outdoor terrace and pool area, creating an effortless indoor-outdoor lifestyle. Large openings allow abundant natural light throughout the property while framing picturesque valley views and lush tropical landscapes.
