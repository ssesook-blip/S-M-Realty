The main home delivers thoughtfully designed living areas, while a detached office/guest suite — complete with its own full ensuite bath — brings the total to 5 bedrooms and 4.5 baths overall. Perfect as a private guest retreat, home office, or separate rental unit, the detached suite adds rare flexibility to the floor plan, and the garage adds further everyday convenience beyond the usable living area.

The monthly maintenance fee includes the following — garbage pickup, maintenance of the community's shared spaces, and round-the-clock guarded access at the gate.
