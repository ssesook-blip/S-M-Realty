Along with its diverse guest accommodations, the property also features a two-bedroom owner’s unit with direct beach views and a rooftop terrace—perfect for private use or as an additional high-value rental option.

Spread across a 1,022 m² beachfront lot with 836 m² of built space, this property blends prime location, established operations, and multiple revenue streams—all in the heart of Cabarete’s thriving water-sports and tourism scene.

WHY KITE BEACH?

- Kite Beach isn’t just a location—it’s a global brand. Known as the kiteboarding capital of the Caribbean, this lively stretch of coastline attracts water-sports enthusiasts, digital nomads, families, and travelers from all over the world.

- Here, the horizon fills daily with colorful kites while the beach buzzes with surf schools, cafés, and an international community that thrives on adventure. Beyond kiteboarding, guests enjoy:

- Consistent year-round trade winds for reliable conditions

- Nearby Playa Encuentro, the Dominican Republic’s premier surf break

- A vibrant mix of restaurants, bars, and nightlife just minutes away in Cabarete town

- A welcoming expat and local community that blends cultures and lifestyles

- For investors, owning a hotel here means securing a front-row seat in one of the Caribbean’s most in-demand tourism hubs, where sports, lifestyle, and steady rental demand intersect.
