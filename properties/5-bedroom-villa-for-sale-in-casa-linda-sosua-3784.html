Discover an exceptional 5-bedroom villa for sale in Casa Linda, one of Sosúa's most sought-after gated communities on the Dominican Republic's North Coast. Set on a sprawling 1,315 m² lot with 372 m² of construction, this two-level villa combines private-residence comfort with serious income potential — a rare find in the Sosúa–Cabarete real estate market.

Each of the five bedrooms has its own private entrance, and the layout includes a main residence plus an independent apartment, giving owners multiple flexible rental configurations: the full 5-bedroom villa, a 1-bedroom rental, a 2-bedroom rental, or any combination in between. It's a setup built for strong Airbnb and long-term rental income, whether you're buying as a full-time home, a second home, or a pure investment property.

Inside, an open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area flows out to spacious terraces on both levels, all built dramatically against a natural 40-foot rock wall. Outside, enjoy a private swimming pool, a natural rock waterfall with water features, a tropical landscaped garden with mature fruit trees, an outdoor bar and entertainment area, and covered parking for up to 4 vehicles. The villa is sold fully furnished and equipped, ready for immediate occupancy or rental.

As part of the Casa Linda community, residents enjoy 24-hour gated security, the Waterworks Waterpark and lazy river, an adult-only pool, Castaway's Clubhouse & Eatery, tennis and pickleball courts, bocce ball, shuffleboard, mini-golf, walking trails, and a complimentary community shuttle — all backed by HOA-managed maintenance.

Location is a major selling point: Casa Linda sits conveniently between Sosúa and Cabarete, just 5–10 minutes from Sosúa Beach and Playa Alicia, and 15–20 minutes from Cabarete's Kite Beach and watersports scene, with exclusive owner discounts at a private beach club. Encuentro Beach (~15 minutes) is a top surf spot, El Choco National Park is adjacent, and Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP) is only 15–20 minutes away. Supermarkets (Supermercado Playero, La Sirena) and medical centers (CMC, Centro Médico Bournigal) are all nearby.

Whether you're a family seeking a secure full-time residence, a buyer looking for a luxury second home, an investor chasing high rental returns, or a retiree wanting resort-style living, this Casa Linda villa delivers a rare combination of privacy, amenities, and income flexibility in one of the Dominican Republic's most in-demand vacation markets.
