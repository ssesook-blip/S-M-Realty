After the stunning success of the original project (now completely sold out!), Mareal 2 is here—bringing the same iconic design and community-forward vibe to a brand-new phase on Kite Beach. Expected delivery: February 2026.

This new development continues the signature Mareal style: soft architectural lines, vertical gardens, and natural textures like limestone and wood, all designed to echo the surrounding tropical beauty. Sleek, modern, and effortlessly relaxed—this is beachfront living with purpose.

Just like phase one, the layout is intentional: two low-rise angled buildings to maximize ocean views, natural light, and breeze flow. Residences range from stylish 1-bedrooms to expansive penthouses with rooftop terraces and private jacuzzis. Some units are set closer to the street, keeping you tapped into the heart of Kite Beach life—not hidden behind gates.

And the amenities? Next level. Think 27-meter pool, 12-person jacuzzi, on-site spa, gym, coworking café, and open-air gathering spaces. Whether you're planning to live here full-time, rent it out, or split your time—Mareal 2 is a solid lifestyle + investment move.

WELCOME TO KITE BEACH. With 270+ windy days a year, this stretch of coast is a world-class destination for kiteboarding, wing foiling, and surf. Mornings bring clean waves perfect for SUP, longboard sessions, or lazy ocean dips. By noon, the sky fills with kites and the beach comes alive.

This is home turf for Dominican kite legends like Adeuri Corniel, Alex Soto, Jose Luis Ciriaco, and Joselito Rosario—all raised right here.

And even if you’re not a rider, you’ll love the vibe. Kite Beach is more intimate and community-driven than Cabarete Bay, with a few laid-back beachfront restaurants, no chain stores, and plenty of friendly faces.

A COMMUNITY THAT FEELS LIKE FAMILY. What makes this place special isn’t just the beach—it’s the people. Kite Beach attracts a global mix of kiters, entrepreneurs, creatives, families, retirees, and travelers who come here for the lifestyle... and end up staying.

This is your chance to be part of it.
