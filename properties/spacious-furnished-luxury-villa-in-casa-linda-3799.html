3 Bedroom 3 ½ Bath in Casa Linda:

Discover this beautifully upgraded 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom villa located in the highly sought-after Casa Linda community in Sosúa, Dominican Republic. Designed for comfortable Caribbean living, this spacious home sits on a larger lot and offers an impressive selection of upgrades throughout the property.

The villa features an extended kitchen and living room, creating a bright and spacious open-concept living area perfect for entertaining or relaxing with family and friends. The outdoor living space has also been enhanced with an extended patio in both width and depth, along with additional patio space extending toward the outbuilding.

Enjoy a larger private swimming pool, complemented by an Eco Serve two-speed high-efficiency pool pump and a convenient pool pump timer. The home also includes upgraded patio ceiling fans with remote controls, providing added comfort while enjoying the beautiful tropical surroundings.

The kitchen and foyer feature Canadian-made light fixtures, while the home includes quality Moen faucets imported from Canada throughout, with a Price Pfister kitchen faucet. Additional upgrades include upgraded floor tile, quartz countertops in the bathrooms, upgraded bathroom hardware, and double vanities in the ensuite.

The spacious primary bedroom has been enhanced with an added walk-in closet with a door, offering additional privacy and storage. Other practical upgrades include air conditioning in the kitchen and living room, a dedicated storage room, a whole-house surge protector, an Eco Serve water filtration system, and a water softener.

Casa Linda Community Amenities

Casa Linda is a gated villa community offering a relaxed Caribbean lifestyle with a wide range of amenities and services. Residents and guests can enjoy 24/7 security, a staffed Welcome Center, community transportation options, restaurants and social spaces, recreational facilities, and family-friendly amenities.

The community features Waterworks, Casa Linda's popular water and recreation area, along with swimming facilities, a poolside bar, tennis and pickleball courts, mini golf, walking areas, playgrounds, and regular community activities and events.

Conveniently located near the beaches, restaurants, supermarkets, and entertainment of Sosúa and Cabarete, Casa Linda offers an excellent combination of privacy, security, community living, and convenience.

This upgraded villa is an excellent opportunity for those looking for a spacious full-time residence, vacation home, or investment property in one of Sosúa's most established and desirable villa communities.
