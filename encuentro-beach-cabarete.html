Encuentro Beach, Cabarete

Playa Encuentro, about 5 km west of Cabarete, is the North Coast's best-known surf beach, with waves for every level most of the year. The area around it has become one of the busiest spots for new villa developments.

What Encuentro is like

Encuentro is about surf and nature: a long beach backed by trees, several reef and beach breaks, surf schools, and a relaxed, low-rise feel. It's quieter than Cabarete town, which is a few minutes' drive east, and it draws surfers, families and remote workers who want space and a slower pace near the water.

What's for sale

Most current listings are new villas in gated developments where you choose your lot and model, plus surf-inspired townhomes, a few oceanfront condos and larger luxury villas. As with any pre-construction purchase, review the land title, permits, payment milestones and delivery date before committing.

Things to check

  • Distance to the beach: walking distance vs. a short drive makes a big difference to lifestyle and rental appeal.
  • Development stage: what's built today vs. what's planned.
  • HOA and services: security, water, power backup and road maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Playa Encuentro?

Playa Encuentro is about 5 km west of Cabarete's town centre on the North Coast of the Dominican Republic.

Is Encuentro good for surfing?

Yes. It's one of the most consistent surf beaches in the region, with breaks for beginners through advanced surfers and several surf schools.

What kind of property is for sale in Encuentro?

Mostly new villas in gated developments, plus townhomes, some oceanfront condos and larger luxury villas.
