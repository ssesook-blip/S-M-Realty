Why buyers choose Sosúa

Sosúa sits on a sheltered bay about fifteen minutes east of Puerto Plata's international airport (POP). Its main beach is protected by an offshore reef, so the water is calm, clear and good for swimming year round, which isn't true of every beach on this coast. Over the decades it has built one of the largest international communities in the Dominican Republic, so English is widely spoken and everyday life is easy to set up: supermarkets with imported goods, banks, private clinics, international schools, gyms and a wide choice of restaurants.

That combination of convenience and beach makes Sosúa the most popular choice for retirees, families relocating full time and buyers who want a vacation home they can also rent out.

Sosúa's neighbourhoods at a glance

  • El Batey: the walkable town centre, a few blocks from Sosúa Beach, with condos and smaller homes. Best for buyers who don't want to rely on a car.
  • Los Charamicos: across the bay, the more traditional Dominican side of town.
  • Playa Chiquita and the coast road east: quieter residential streets close to small beaches, plus large gated communities like Sosúa Ocean Village.
  • Gated communities in the hills: Casa Linda, Hispaniola, Panorama Village and others, with security, shared amenities and villas with private pools.
  • El Choco: greener, quieter hillside land between Sosúa and Cabarete, popular for new construction and larger lots.

What you can buy

Sosúa has the widest range on the North Coast. One-bedroom condos in town start around the low $100,000s. Villas with a private pool in gated communities usually run from the high $200,000s to $600,000. Luxury and oceanfront homes go well beyond that. There's also a steady supply of new construction, often with developer payment plans, and a good number of resale properties with owner financing.

Before you buy, look closely at monthly HOA fees, the community's short-term rental rules, and how the property handles power and water (inverters, generators and cisterns are common). Our guide for foreign buyers covers the legal checks.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sosúa a good place to buy property?

For many buyers, yes. Sosúa combines a calm swimming beach, a large international community and everyday services such as supermarkets, banks, clinics and international schools, about 15 minutes from Puerto Plata's international airport.

How much does a house in Sosúa cost?

Condos in town start around the low $100,000s, villas with private pools in gated communities typically run from the high $200,000s to around $600,000, and luxury or oceanfront homes cost more. See the current listings on this page for live prices.

Can foreigners buy property in Sosúa?

Yes. Foreign buyers can own titled property in the Dominican Republic in their own name, with generally the same rights as Dominican citizens. Use an independent attorney for due diligence.
