El Choco, Sosúa

El Choco is the green, hilly area inland from Sosúa and Cabarete, next to the El Choco National Park. It's where buyers go for more land, more privacy and new construction, a short drive from the beaches.

What El Choco is like

The hills of El Choco feel much more rural than the coast below. You get mature trees, cooler breezes, mountain and valley views, and bigger lots, often with room for a guest house or a garden. The neighbouring El Choco National Park protects lagoons, mangroves and a network of caves south of Cabarete, so the area keeps its green character.

You'll need a car to get around, but the beaches, supermarkets and schools of Sosúa and Cabarete are a short drive away.

What's for sale

El Choco is one of the most active areas for new construction around Sosúa. Expect small gated developments of contemporary villas with private pools, custom-built family homes on large lots, and land, including larger parcels suited to builders and developers.

Things to check

  • Access roads: ask how the road performs in heavy rain.
  • Utilities: confirm power, water supply and internet options for the specific lot.
  • New builds: review the developer's permits, payment schedule and delivery date. See our buyer's guide.

Frequently asked questions

Where is El Choco?

El Choco is the hilly area inland from the coast between Sosúa and Cabarete, next to El Choco National Park.

Is El Choco good for building a home?

Yes. It's one of the main areas for new construction around Sosúa, with larger lots, privacy and views. Check road access and utilities for each specific lot.

Do I need a car in El Choco?

Yes. El Choco is a short drive from Sosúa and Cabarete, but not within walking distance of shops or beaches.
