How It Works

Selling with us: four stages.

One point of contact from the first conversation to the day you sign.

I

Valuation

We look at your property in person or from your photos and details, compare it with what's for sale and what has sold nearby, and agree an asking price with you.

II

Preparing the listing

Frequently asked questions

Is the valuation free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to list your property with us.

How do you work out what my property is worth?

We compare your property with similar homes for sale and recently sold in the same community or neighbourhood, then adjust for size, condition, views, distance to the beach, HOA fees and rental history. Asking prices alone can be misleading, so we look at what buyers are actually paying.

What paperwork will I need to sell?

Usually your certificate of title (Certificado de Título), your ID or passport, proof that property taxes and HOA fees are up to date, and any plans or permits for work done on the property. If anything is missing, we'll tell you early so it doesn't hold up a sale.

Can I sell while I'm living outside the Dominican Republic?

Yes. Many of our sellers live abroad. You can sign through a power of attorney, so you don't need to fly in for the closing.

Will I pay tax when I sell?

There is a tax on the gain from a property sale, with some exemptions. Our closing costs and property tax guide explains the current rules. Confirm your own situation with a tax advisor before you sell.
